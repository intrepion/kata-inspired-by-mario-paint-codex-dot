# Glossary

**Project**: one connected creative work containing a painting, four animation frames, four stamps and a score.

**Painting**: the opaque background picture shared with the flipbook.

**Frame**: a transparent picture shown over the painting during animation.

**Stamp**: a small reusable print with transparent cells.

**Beat**: one score position, holding at most three notes.

**Sound character**: the icon identifying a synthesized voice.
